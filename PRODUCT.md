# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Meloday is for people who use music to regulate emotions, record daily feelings, meditate, sleep, or move with more ease. They usually open the app in a quiet personal moment and need fast, gentle guidance rather than a dense productivity tool.

## Product Purpose

Meloday turns emotional check-ins into music-centered healing moments and keeps those moments as a diary. Success means users can quickly choose the right mode, feel accompanied by the app, and return to saved diary entries without friction.

## Brand Personality

Soothing, light, and fox-like cheeky. The product should feel calm and premium, but not sterile; the mascot can bring small sparks of personality without making the interface childish.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic meditation-app beige minimalism, noisy gamified wellness dashboards, overly cute babyish mascots, and decorative UI that distracts from listening or journaling.

## Design Principles

- Quiet, not empty: use calm surfaces with enough structure to make choices obvious.
- Clear mode choice first: the home screen should explain the four listening modes at a glance.
- Companion, not cartoon show: the fox adds warmth and wit without taking over the task.
- Restrained motion: feedback should be quick and stateful, never theatrical.
- Readability first: soft colors must still keep text and controls easy to read.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target readable contrast, 44px minimum touch targets, semantic labels for icon-only controls, and reduced-motion alternatives for decorative transitions.
